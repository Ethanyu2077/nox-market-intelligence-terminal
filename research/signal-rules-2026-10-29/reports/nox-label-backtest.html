Per-token samples are small, and each token's baseline reflects its own trend that week. DOOD fell, so its bearish labels and bearish baseline both score high. No single token shows a clear, consistent edge across horizons.

Why the labels underperform

  • The thresholds are too small for these tokens. A label needs only a 0.05% OI change and a 0.08% price move in 5 minutes. These tokens moved 1.3% to 4.7% in an average hour, so more than half of all bars got a label and most of them tag noise.
  • The confidence score isn't calibrated. It is a fixed formula of OI score, price move and flow agreement, and higher scores were not more accurate here. The code itself marks it heuristic-rule-uncalibrated.
  • Same-bar momentum doesn't carry forward. Each label describes the bar that just closed. On these tokens, the next move reversed that bar's direction slightly more often than it continued it.

Method

  • Data: Binance USDT-M /fapi/v1/klines and /futures/data/openInterestHist at 5m, with 300 extra earlier bars so NOX's baselines had history. All five tokens returned complete data.
  • Labels: generated by NOX's own code, computeOiDeltas and detectPositionFlowSignals in server/calculations.ts, unmodified.
  • Flow: taken from each candle's taker-buy volume, which uses the same buyer-maker rule as the trade feed. Every bucket is therefore complete. This tests the label rules; the live app sees only the latest 1,000 trades and would withhold most of these bars.
  • Scoring: entry at the open of the signal bar, exit at the close 15m, 60m or 4h later, the convention NOX's own backtest uses. A hit means price moved the way the label implies. The baseline applies the same scoring to every bar. Cost is 10 bps per round trip (about 5 bps taker fee per side).

Limits

  • Seven days is a single market period, and these five small-cap tokens tend to move together.
  • Signals at 60m and 4h overlap heavily, so they are not independent samples.
  • Assumes the OI value at each timestamp was known at that time, and that entry fills exactly at the bar open. Funding and slippage are not included.
  • Covers only the position-flow labels. Regime V2 and timeframe-alignment claims depend on NOX's own stored history and were not tested.
