NOX Signal Rule Walk-Forward: IDOL, DOS, JCT, DOOD, EVAA

New label rules make NOX label fewer bars and behave the same in calm and volatile weeks. They still don't predict price. On 9 days the rules never saw, the best variant from training was right 52.5% of the time against a 49.5% baseline, but its return after costs is indistinguishable from zero.

Summary

Recommendation: keep the new rules opt-in. They fix how often labels fire, which is a real improvement. They don't add a measurable edge, so the app should stay on its current defaults until better inputs (funding, liquidations, cross-exchange confirmation, longer history) are tested.

What the results show

  • Labelling is steadier. The fixed thresholds labelled 27% of bars in training and 52% in testing because volatility rose. Adaptive thresholds scale with each token, so the 5m adaptive variants labelled about 11% of bars in both periods.
  • Accuracy barely moves. The selected variant beat its baseline by 3 points on the test days, but t = 0.10 is noise, it came from picking the best of 18 variants, and it turns negative with a one-bar entry delay.
  • No variant reached t ≥ 2 on the test days, positive or negative, on non-overlapping signals. Training performance did not predict test performance (rank correlation 0.10).
  • The 60m-window variants label about 40% of bars because each bar's window overlaps the previous one, so neighbouring labels are near-duplicates. The non-overlapping columns account for this.

What changed in NOX

detectPositionFlowSignals in server/calculations.ts takes an optional fifth argument. Without it, labels are exactly the same as before.

OptionWhat it doesDefault
adaptiveA move must exceed the token's own q-quantile of earlier windows. Too little history gives no label, never a fallback.off
minFlowImbalancePctFlow must be at least this one-sided: (buy − sell) / (buy + sell).0
windowStepsMeasures price, OI change and flow over several 5m steps.1
limitHow many recent labels to return.80
  • Invalid settings (NaN, out of range, non-integer windows) are rejected with an error.
  • Fixed a bug where a bar with zero flow counted as "absorption" whenever recent flow was all zero. Absorption is always withheld in the app, so nothing visible changes.
  • Six new tests cover unchanged defaults, adaptive thresholds, no rewriting of earlier labels when later data arrives, the imbalance rule, wider windows and invalid settings.
  • Full suite: 196 tests, 193 pass, 0 fail, 3 skipped (opt-in live-provider tests). Lint and typecheck pass. On the 7-day data, the defaults reproduce all 6,073 original labels exactly.
  • The app itself (server/binance.ts) still calls the function with its defaults.

Method

  • Data: Binance /fapi/v1/klines and /futures/data/openInterestHist at 5m, 28 days plus one day of warm-up. That's 8,352 bars per token, all complete. Binance's public OI history only reaches back about 30 days.
  • Flow: each candle's taker-buy volume, which uses the same buyer-maker rule as the trade feed, so every bucket is complete. This tests the rules, not the live app's 1,000-trade window.
  • Selection rule, fixed before running: the variant with the best non-overlapping 60m after-cost return on the training days, with at least 100 samples. Only that variant's test result counts as the outcome.
  • Scoring: entry at the open of the signal bar, exit at the close 15m, 60m or 4h later. A hit means price moved the way the label implies. The baseline applies the same scoring to every bar in the period. Cost is 10 bps per round trip.

Limits

  • 28 days and 5 small-cap tokens that tend to move together; the test period is only 9 days.
  • 18 variants were compared, so the best training result is partly luck.
  • Assumes OI at each timestamp was known at that time and that entry fills at the bar open. Funding and slippage are not included.
  • Covers only the position-flow labels, not Regime V2 or timeframe alignment.
